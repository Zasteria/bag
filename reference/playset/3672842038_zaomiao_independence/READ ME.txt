[h1]为什么要做这个mod？[/h1]
- 现版本的原版游戏中，如果一个附庸国针对其宗主国创建了一个独立运动组织，一些与他的宗主国毫无关系且距离遥远的其他大国就很容易加入这个独立运动组织。而在这些大国加入之后，附庸国就会发起独立战争。在独立战争中，由于宗主国与参与这个独立运动组织的那些大国相隔遥远，他们之间完全没有办法互相占领土地和交战，这会导致这场荒谬的独立战争迟迟无法结束。
[hr][/hr]
[h1]这个Mod具体具有什么效果？[/h1]
本mod作了如下改动：如果你是一个独立国家，你必须对独立运动组织针对的宗主国有至少50的敌意，你才能够加入这个独立运动组织，以帮助附庸国。
[hr][/hr]
[h1]这个mod会破坏游戏平衡性吗？[/h1]
- 并不会，不如说这反而会让游戏更加合理。

[h1]这个mod对ai管用吗？会不会只是加强了玩家？[/h1]
- 由于本mod的效果均通过更改底层代码实现，因此对于ai和玩家都是平等的。

Fix the Endless Independence War

[h1]Recommended Other Mods by the Author:[/h1]

[h1]Why was this mod made?[/h1]
-In the current version of the base game, if a subject nation creates an Independence Movement against its overlord, other major powers—who have no relation to the overlord and are geographically distant—can easily join this Independence Movement. Once these major powers join, the subject nation will declare an independence war. During the war, because the overlord and the major powers participating in this Independence Movement are far apart, they are completely unable to occupy each other's lands or engage in direct conflict. This leads to an absurd and prolonged independence war that drags on indefinitely.
[hr][/hr]
[h1]What specific effects does this Mod have?[/h1]
-This mod makes the following change: if you are an independent nation, you must have at least 50 Antagonism toward the overlord targeted by the Independence Movement in order to join this Independence Movement and assist the subject nation.
[hr][/hr]
[h1]Does this mod break game balance?[/h1]
-Not at all. In fact, it makes the game more reasonable.
[h1]Does this mod work for the AI? Does it only benefit the player?[/h1]
-Since the effects of this mod are achieved by modifying the underlying code, they apply equally to both the AI and the player.
