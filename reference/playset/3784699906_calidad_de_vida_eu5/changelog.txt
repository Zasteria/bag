Quality of Life by Buddy - Changelog
=====================================

v1.0.1 (2026-08-19)
--------------------
- Fixed: Cooldown alerts for estate emergency interactions now work correctly.
  The cooldown type names in the watch list now match the vanilla cooldown
  identifiers (e.g. ask_for_extra_levies_cooldown).
  Affected interactions: Ask for Extra Levies, Levy Extraordinary Taxes,
  Request Clerical Endorsement, Request Noble Envoys, Request Emergency Grant,
  Appeal to the Commoners, Call the Tribes to Arms, Call Emergency Parliament.
