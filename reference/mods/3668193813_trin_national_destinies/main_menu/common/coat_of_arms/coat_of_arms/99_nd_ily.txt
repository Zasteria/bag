# Illyria: azure, three leopards' heads crowned or, within a bordure checky
# gules and argent. No authentic period Illyrian flag exists, so nothing is
# invented and nothing is borrowed from 1809 or the 1830s. Both charges are
# taken straight from the heraldry of the two polities the word actually meant

# Dalmatia (vanilla DAL), and the red and white chequerboard is the Croatian
# sahovnica (vanilla CRO), here reduced to a bordure so it frames Dalmatia
# rather than replacing it. Reads at a glance as neither parent.
